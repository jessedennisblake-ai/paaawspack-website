4. WARRANTY POLICY

4.1 Standard Warranty (Free – 6 Months)

What's Covered:

  • LED board malfunction

  • Screen defects (e.g., dead pixels, display failure)

  • Electronic failure not caused by the user

What's NOT Covered:

  • Drops or impact damage

  • Water or liquid exposure

  • Damage from overcharging or incorrect charging methods

  • Unauthorised repairs or modifications

4.2 Warranty Claim Process

To make a warranty claim, you must provide:

  • Proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt)

  • Video evidence clearly showing the fault

  • Proof of delivery date

The faulty board must be returned to us intact. Once we have checked it and confirmed the fault was not caused by the user, the Company will issue a replacement free of charge.

To make claims quicker, register your board in the PAAAWS PACK app (PAAAWS Extras → Register your board) or on WhatsApp.

4.3 Protection+ Extended Cover (£50/year)

  • Covers up to 4 LED board replacements in the 12 months after purchase, for accidental damage or manufacturer defects. Your board number is needed at checkout.

  • Customer must return the damaged board within 14 days of receiving the replacement.

  • If the damaged board is not returned within 14 days, no further replacements will be provided.

  • Protection+ is a single payment and does not renew automatically. You can buy it again for another 12 months.

4.3a Replacement Screen (£15)

  • If your board is damaged and not covered by the standard warranty or Protection+, you can buy a replacement screen for £15.

4.4 Fraud Prevention

Warranty will be cancelled immediately if:

  • A customer attempts to use someone else's damaged board to claim a replacement

  • Fake or misleading claims are submitted
